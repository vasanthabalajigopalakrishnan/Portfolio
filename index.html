The problem

What I did

04 · Strategy & analysis

Growth strategy and competitive benchmarking

SAP sales and billing data
SQL · Excel · Power BI
Executive presentation

The problem

Ownership needed an evidence-based view of where growth could come from and how exposed the business was to its largest accounts.

What I did

Analyzed 20+ months of SAP sales and billing data across three plants for revenue trends, customer concentration and backlog visibility.
